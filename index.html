How solar works

From sunshine to savings in four easy steps.

Step 1

Sun hits your panels

High-efficiency panels capture sunlight — even on cloudy days.

Step 2

Inverter converts it

A smart inverter turns DC power into clean AC electricity.

Step 3

Your home runs on solar

Appliances draw free solar power first, before touching the grid.

Step 4

Store or sell the rest

Extra energy charges your battery or exports to the grid for credit.
